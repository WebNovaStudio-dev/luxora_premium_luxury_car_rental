Frequently Asked

Common Inquiries

What are the age and license requirements to rent with LUXORA?

Drivers must be at least 21 years of age for Luxury Sedans and SUVs, and 25 years old for Supercars and track-tier models. A valid international or domestic driver's license held for at least 2 years is required alongside identity verification.

Is comprehensive insurance included in the daily rental quote?

Yes. All LUXORA rentals include premium comprehensive collision and liability coverage with low deductible tiers. Additional zero-excess protection and glass/tire waivers are optionally available during booking.

Can the vehicle be delivered directly to my private jet terminal or villa?

Absolutely. We provide bespoke white-glove doorstep delivery directly to private FBO airfields, five-star resorts, yacht harbors, and residences within our operational service radii.
